Leads Hub Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Divigo India Private Limited

In short: Leads Hub is a lead-management app for businesses and their sales teams. We collect what is needed to run your account and the leads your business manages, we use it only to provide the service, and we never sell it. You can delete your account in the app at any time under Settings › Delete my account.

1. Who we are

Leads Hub is provided by Divigo India Private Limited ("we", "us"), 203, Heritage Manor, Methodist Colony, Begumpet, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. This policy covers the Leads Hub apps for iPhone and Android and the related web service. Questions: admin@snap.pe.

2. Who uses Leads Hub

Leads Hub is used by businesses ("your business") and the people they add as users. Accounts are created by your business's admin; there is no public sign-up. Your business decides what lead information it stores in Leads Hub, and we process that information on its behalf.

3. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, mobile number, your business, and your role in it.
  • Lead and customer information your business stores: names, phone numbers, email addresses, locations, categories, follow-up dates, notes and files attached to a lead.
  • Lead marketplace activity: leads your business takes from the marketplace, orders, and the credits your admin assigns and uses. Credits are an internal budget set by your business; Leads Hub does not take card, bank or UPI payments.
  • Files you choose to attach: when you attach a photo or document to a lead's notes, the app reads only the file you pick.
  • Technical information: device type, operating system, app version, IP address and error logs, used to keep the service secure and working.

The Leads Hub iPhone app does not access your contacts, call history, location, camera or microphone, and it contains no advertising or third-party analytics trackers.

4. How we use information

  • to provide Leads Hub: sign-in, showing and updating leads, follow-ups, users, dashboards and the lead marketplace;
  • to keep accounts secure, prevent misuse and fix problems;
  • to send messages about your account or the service (for example sign-in codes and important changes);
  • to meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Who we share it with

  • Your business: admins and permitted users of your business can see the leads and activity in it.
  • Service providers who host and operate the service for us (such as cloud hosting, file storage and message delivery), only as needed to run Leads Hub and under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account is active. Lead information is kept for as long as your business keeps it in Leads Hub or until it deletes it. When an account is deleted, we remove it from active use; limited records may be kept where the law requires (for example billing or security records), and backups are overwritten over time.

7. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: Settings › Delete my account. Your user account in your business is deleted and you are signed out. Leads and notes you added stay with your business, which owns them.
  • By email: write to admin@snap.pe from your registered email to ask for your account or personal information to be deleted, corrected or shared with you.
  • A business can delete its leads in the app at any time, or ask us to delete all of its data.

8. Security

Connections to Leads Hub are encrypted (HTTPS), access is limited by role, and sign-in uses your password or a one-time code. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Children

Leads Hub is a business tool and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Where information is stored

Information is processed in India and may be stored with our hosting providers in other locations, with appropriate safeguards.

11. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or raise a concern, by writing to admin@snap.pe. We reply within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above, and tell you in the app for important changes.
