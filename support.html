Common questions

  • How do I get an account? Leads Hub accounts are created by your company's admin. Ask your admin to add you as a user.
  • I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen choose Sign in with OTP instead to get a one-time code, or ask your admin to reset your access.
  • How do credits work? Credits are a budget your company's admin assigns to you for taking leads from the marketplace. Ask your admin if you need more.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app go to Settings › Delete my account, or email us from your registered email address.
